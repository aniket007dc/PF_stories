REEL WRITER · PF Guru Ji

Turn the approved script below into the reel's shot list and its build file. tools/build_reel.py draws everything from your answer, in the style of the reference reel: paper, header, captions and sound are added for you. Be exact: every word on screen must come from that shot's card_text, every number from the episode's data, and every form name from brand/epfo-forms.json.

ANSWER WITH JSON ONLY, in three parts:
{"shots": [...], "breaks": [...], "reel": {"photos": {...}, "shots": {...}}}

1. "shots": one per script line, with the same ids, in the same order:
{"id": "S01", "start": 0, "end": 4, "chapter": "hook", "on_screen": "a short description for people", "card_text": ["every word or number on screen when the shot ends, exactly as drawn"], "motion_text": [], "uses": ["the data ids of every number in the line or on screen"], "yellow_elements": 1}
- Times: the first shot starts at 0 and each shot starts where the last one ended. Make each shot about (words in its line ÷ 2.6) + 0.5 seconds, rounded to 0.5. Give the last shot 3 extra seconds for the end card. The total must be 60–95 seconds; aim for about 70. (The build re-times every shot to the real voice later.)
- card_text: every string drawn in the shot, exactly. Numbers come only from the shot's "uses" data, with the ₹ sign and Indian commas (₹50,000, 1,00,000). The first card that shows an example number (a data item of kind "assumption") must say MAAN LO or EXAMPLE. Write forms as FORM 19, FORM 10C.
- motion_text: strings that are drawn but leave before the shot ends, such as a 3 WAJAH headline that slides away. They go here, not in card_text (the check reads card_text at the end of the shot). Usually empty.
- yellow_elements: how many yellow cards or tags the shot has. Never more than 1.

2. "breaks": the two torn-paper sheets that sweep across between chapters:
[{"after": "<last shot of the why chapter>", "at": <that shot's end time>, "type": "tear-lemon", "asset": "A08"},
 {"after": "<last shot of the check chapter>", "at": <that shot's end time>, "type": "tear-paper", "asset": "A09"}]

3. "reel": the build file.
- "photos": every photo the reel uses (2 to 4), as {"<short_id>": {"id": "P1", "file": "episodes/{{EPISODE}}/media/photos/<short_id>.webp", "alt": "what it shows, in a few words", "prompt": "..."}}. Write each prompt like this: "Black-and-white documentary photograph, landscape 4:3. <who or what, doing what, where in India>. <the light>, high contrast, fine film grain, eye-level camera, the subject in the middle of the frame. No text, no readable labels, no logos, no watermark." Never ask for signs, screens with writing, documents or forms with readable text, ID cards, passbooks, the EPFO logo or any government emblem.
- "screens": only for a reel that shows the member portal: every screenshot it uses, as {"<short_id>": {"id": "SC1", "file": "episodes/{{EPISODE}}/media/screens/<short_id>.png", "shows": "which page and what is open on it, for the person taking it", "must_show": ["each menu or button name the voice says on that screen, written as the portal writes it"]}}. A person takes them on their own login, with every personal detail covered; once one is uploaded, add "crop" ([x0, y0, x1, y1], shares of the image) to show only the part the line is about.
- "shots": {"S01": [elements], "S02": [elements], ...}: what each shot shows and when, built only from the blocks below.

BUILDING BLOCKS
Positions are pixels on the 1080×1920 frame. "x" is the horizontal centre (540 is the middle) and "y" is the top. Stamps and notes use their centre (x, y). Everything goes between y = 400 and y = 1240: the header is above and the captions are below.
Timing: "at" is seconds after the shot starts (the first element 0.1; the reel's very first element 0). "on" is a word from the shot's voice line (vo), copied with its punctuation if it has any ("Reject.", "Ek:"): the element lands as that word is spoken. Use "on" for every element after the first.
Entrances ("in"): rise (cards, the default), drop (photos and row cards), slide_left or slide_right (arrow cards and tags), slam (stamps, the default), pop (headlines, the default), write (notes, the default), none.
Tilt ("rot", degrees): cards −1.5 to 1.5, stamps 3 to 8 either way, notes −4 to −2.

- photo: {"type": "photo", "src": "<short_id>", "tab": "TAB TEXT", "y": 400, "w": 960, "h": 560, "rot": -1.2}
  A halftone photo with torn top and bottom edges. "tab" is optional: a small black label at the bottom left.
- card: {"type": "card", "style": "white|yellow|black", "tab": "TAB TEXT", "prefix": "=", "big": "₹50,000", "label": "PF BALANCE\n(MAAN LO)", "foot": "small print", "size": 120, "y": 430, "w": 900, "h": 470}
  Big-number card: yellow, a tab, "big" at size 200–250, an optional "foot", h 400–470.
  Row cards, in a stack of 2 or 3: white (the result yellow), "prefix" (÷ × = ≈ +) and "big" at size 120 and a "label", no tab, at y 420, 595, 770.
  Black card: a tab (drawn in yellow) and "big" in white, w 600–760, overlapping the bottom edge of a photo.
  Its card_text strings are: the tab; the prefix and big joined by a space ("= ₹50,000"); the label on one line (line breaks become spaces); the foot.
- stamp: {"type": "stamp", "text": "REJECT!", "color": "red|ink", "x": 900, "y": 735, "rot": 8, "size": 72}
  A short verdict: REJECT!, PEHLE YEH, FREE HAI. Red, or "ink" (black) for the good side of a comparison. Never on top of small print.
- headline: {"type": "headline", "lines": ["CLAIM", "REJECT?"], "x": 80, "y": 520, "size": 260, "underline": {"line": 1, "at": 1.0, "dur": 0.5}}
  Huge words straight on the paper, for a question or a turn: 1 or 2 lines of 1 to 3 words. card_text has each line, or the lines joined by a space. "underline" is optional (a yellow marker line with a loop).
- note: {"type": "note", "text": "employer se poochho", "color": "red|ink", "x": 320, "y": 1110, "size": 74, "rot": -3}
  A handwritten aside of 2 to 5 lowercase words.
- tag: {"type": "tag", "text": "SHARE ↗", "x": 760, "y": 905, "rot": -4, "size": 96}
  A yellow slap-on label of 1 to 3 words, over a photo's lower edge.
- funnel: {"type": "funnel", "tab": "CLAIM KA SAFAR", "y": 420, "w": 920, "rows": [{"text": "CLAIM FILED", "width": 1.0, "on": "file"}, {"text": "KYC CHECK", "width": 0.76, "on": "KYC"}, {"text": "SETTLED", "width": 0.58, "style": "yellow", "on": "settle"}]}
  Graph-paper card whose bars grow one by one as each stage is spoken.
- line_chart: {"type": "line_chart", "tab": "<WHAT> · <UNIT> · EXAMPLE", "start": {"value": "<first value>", "sub": "<WHEN>"}, "end": {"value": "<last value>", "sub": "<WHEN>"}, "y": 420, "w": 920, "h": 560, "draw_at": 0.6, "draw_dur": 1.5}
  A red line drawing itself from the start value down to the end value. Values come from the data.
- arrow_card: {"type": "arrow_card", "arrow": "→", "title": "BANK KYC", "sub": "VERIFIED NAHI", "style": "white|yellow", "y": 420, "w": 900, "in": "slide_left"}
  For a list of reasons or steps: three cards at y 420, 600 and 780, each "on" its Ek: / Do: / Teen: word, arrows → ↘ ↗, one of them yellow. The arrow symbol is its own card_text string.
- timeline: {"type": "timeline", "tab": "NAUKRI CHHODNE KE BAAD", "segments": [{"from": 0, "to": 0.5, "style": "yellow", "label": "<FIRST STRETCH>", "on": "<word>"}], "ticks": [{"text": "<number from the data>", "pos": 0}], "foot": "<the rule, in a few words>"}
  A bar that fills in segments (yellow, white, red), with tick numbers under it and a key. Use it for waiting times; every tick number comes from the data.
- typewriter: {"type": "typewriter", "tab": "CLAIM SE PEHLE", "lines": ["Bank KYC verified.", "Aadhaar UAN se linked.", "Exit date marked."], "highlight": "verified", "highlight_on": "verified", "type_at": 0.5, "line_every": 1.15}
  A checklist of 2 to 4 lines, at most 26 characters each, typed out with a highlighter swipe.
- screen: {"type": "screen", "src": "<short_id in screens>", "tab": "MEMBER PORTAL", "y": 420, "w": 880, "h": 760, "marks": [{"box": [0.05, 0.20, 0.60, 0.27], "text": "Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)", "on": "Claim"}]}
  A portal screenshot on a white card, for the steps of a portal how-to: one screen per step. Each mark is a red ring (or "style": "lemon", a yellow highlight) that lands on its word, round the menu or button the line names. "box" is in shares of the cropped screenshot and "text" is exactly what's inside it on screen: the check reads the screenshot and gives the right box if it's off. The screenshot's own words aren't card text; only the tab is, so give the tab no number (PORTAL · LOGIN, not STEP 4). Never show anything personal. A screenshot shot stays at least 4.6 seconds ("hold" changes it), so the viewer can find the button.
- endcard: the sign-off shot only, always these two elements:
  {"type": "note", "text": "{{TAGLINE}}", "color": "ink", "x": 540, "y": 760, "size": 124, "rot": -2, "at": 0.1, "dur": 1.0, "out": {"on": "EPFO"}}
  {"type": "endcard", "tagline": "{{TAGLINE}}", "small": ["<the main source, like NIYAM: EPFO.GOV.IN>", "<what's an example, if anything is>", "EPFO KA OFFICIAL CHANNEL NAHI"], "y": 430, "on": "EPFO", "lead": -0.2, "cta_on": "Follow"}
  The end card draws the logo, the tagline, the follow line ("{{FOLLOW_NOTE}}" over a "{{FOLLOW_BUTTON}}" button that pops in on "Follow") and the small print. Its card_text is "{{TAGLINE}}", "{{FOLLOW_NOTE}}", "{{FOLLOW_BUTTON}}" and the small lines.

HOW EACH CHAPTER LOOKS
- hook: a photo of the viewer's world filling the top ("at": 0, "in": "none", so the very first frame is already full), a red stamp with the hook question across it, and a black card naming what's stuck overlapping the photo's bottom edge. Then a headline shot with the question.
- problem: a big yellow card with what the viewer has lost or is waiting for (an amount from the data, or the form they filed), plus a red note with the catch.
- why: the reasons as three arrow cards ("Ek: … Do: … Teen: …"), one of them yellow, and a red stamp on the most common one. If the line starts by counting the reasons, open with a 3 WAJAH headline that slides away ("out": {"at": 1.0}) before the cards come in.
- steps: a typewriter card with the path through the portal, one line per step, or a funnel when the steps happen in order to the claim itself. When the reel shows the member portal, a screenshot per step instead, with a ring on the menu or button the line names.
- check: a big yellow card or a row-card stack with the one thing to check, and a red stamp with what skipping it costs; a timeline when the rule is a waiting time.
- help: a black or white card naming the official channel (portal or helpline, from the data), with an ink stamp FREE HAI or OFFICIAL.
- share: a new photo of a hand holding a phone with a blank screen (a different hand, angle and background from earlier reels), a yellow "SHARE ↗" tag on "abhi" or "bhejo", and a red note naming who to send it to.
- signoff: the endcard pair above.
Each element lands on its own beat of the line: 1 to 5 elements per shot. At most one yellow element per shot. Red only for stamps and notes.

CHECK BEFORE YOU ANSWER
- Every string you draw is in that shot's card_text or motion_text, character for character, and every card_text string is still on screen when the shot ends.
- Every number on screen is in that shot's "uses" data, written with ₹ and Indian commas, and every form named is in brand/epfo-forms.json.
- Every "on" word is in that shot's vo line, and every mark's "text" is a menu or button name its line says.
- Nothing sits above y 400 or below y 1240, and nothing covers small print.
- The photo prompts ask for no writing of any kind, and no documents, ID cards or official logos.
- Every photo prompt is written new for this reel. Never copy a prompt or reuse a photo from another reel's reel.json: the check compares them.
