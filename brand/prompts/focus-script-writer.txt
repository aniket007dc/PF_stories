THE SHAPE (100–220 words, one line per shot; 40 to 120 seconds)
The task decides which parts it needs, in this order:
- hook (S01): the problem or the result in the viewer's own words, in 8 words or fewer for the first sentence.
- why (1 line): what goes wrong without it, or what they get.
- route (1 line): the whole path in one breath before the first step ("Raasta seedha hai: Manage, Mark Exit, OTP, aur check."). Its stops stay on screen as chips while the steps run.
- steps: one line per action, in order, grouped by the route's stops.
- mistake (1 line): the one mistake most people make here, and what it costs.
- fix (1 line): how to avoid it.
- check (1 line): how to see it worked: a status, a date in the passbook, a message.
- list (1 line): the steps as a checklist worth a screenshot ("Yeh list save kar lo, …").
- share (1 line, right before the sign-off): aimed at one kind of friend, ending "… bhejo." Never "tag karo", "like karo" or a number of friends.
- signoff: exactly "{{SIGNOFF}}"
Every line has a "chapter": the part it belongs to (hook, why, route, steps, mistake, fix, check, list, share, signoff), or one short lowercase word of your own for a part this task needs. These names show on screen as: {{PARTS}}.
