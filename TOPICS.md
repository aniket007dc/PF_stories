## ep05-transfer-out-rejected

- **Title:** Transfer out form reject kyun hota hai?
- **Topic:** Transfer out form (Form 13) reject kyun hota hai, aur kaise theek karein?
- **Hook:** Nayi naukri join kar li — purana PF aaya nahi?
- **Covers:** Why: The two jobs' service periods overlap in EPFO's records — EPFO itself says regional offices have been rejecting transfer claims for this. · The member's identity or account ownership couldn't be confirmed online, so the claim goes to the employer to forward after confirming identity. · The pension amount never moves with a transfer — only the past service history does — so a passbook without the pension amount looks like a failed transfer.. Steps: Check the service dates: the old exit date against the new joining date — if they overlap, that is what EPFO's offices have been rejecting claims for. · If the identity couldn't be confirmed online, ask the employer to confirm the identity and forward the application — the Scheme says the employer shall do that. · If the employer won't attest or isn't available, get the form attested by the bank manager and submit it to the Regional PF Commissioner. · Check the transfer with Annexure K — the transfer certificate with the full details.
- **Cover:** PURANA PF ATKA KYUN? FORM 13
- **Photos:** P1 a young man on his first day at a new office desk, staring at his phone with a worried frown · P2 a hand holding out a phone with a blank screen towards a friend

## ep04-epf-interest-vs-fd

- **Title:** EPF ka byaaj FD se zyada? Safe options ki tulna.
- **Topic:** EPF ka byaaj FD, post office, PPF aur LIC se zyada kyun? Safe options ki seedhi tulna.
- **Hook:** PF nikaal ke FD karne ki soch rahe ho? Ruko!
- **Covers:** Why: EPF's 2025-26 rate is higher than SBI's best FD rate, PPF's and NSC's this quarter · An LIC endowment policy has no fixed interest rate: its return comes from bonuses · EPF interest is tax-free on up to ₹2,50,000 of one's own contribution a year; FD interest is taxed. Steps: See what the difference means on one sum (an example ₹1,00,000 for a year, simple interest) · On changing jobs, transfer the PF instead of withdrawing it · Know how EPF interest builds up: on the monthly balance, credited at the year's end
- **Cover:** PF NIKAAL KE FD? RUKO! ₹1,850 / SAAL

## ep03-claim-online-steps

- **Title:** PF claim online kaise bharein? Step by step.
- **Topic:** PF claim online kaise bharein? Member portal pe step by step.
- **Hook:** PF nikalna hai? Agent ki zaroorat nahi.
- **Covers:** Why: The UAN isn't linked to Aadhaar, so the OTP that submits the claim can't be sent · The bank account isn't verified in KYC · For a final settlement, the date of exit isn't marked. Steps: Log in to the member portal with the UAN, password and captcha · Online Services, then Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D); verify the bank account number · Proceed for Online Claim, then pick the claim from the list (an advance also needs its purpose and amount) · Submit with the OTP sent to the Aadhaar-linked mobile; follow it under Track Claim Status

## ep02-what-is-pf

- **Title:** PF kya hai? Salary se kaise kat-ta hai?
- **Topic:** PF kya hai, employee par iska kya asar hota hai, aur salary se PF kaise kat-ta hai?
- **Hook:** Salary aayi, PF kat gaya — yeh paisa kahan gaya?
- **Covers:** Why: PF retirement ke liye bachat hai — EPF Scheme ke tahat employee aur employer dono har mahine contribute karte hain · Contribution Basic + DA par 12% hai, isliye salary slip mein har mahine deduction dikhta hai · Employer ke hisse ka 8.33% pension (EPS) mein jata hai, isliye passbook mein employer ka EPF hissa chhota dikhta hai. Steps: Salary slip mein Basic + DA dekho — PF usi par 12% kat-ta hai · Apna 12% aur company ka 12% passbook mein dekho — maan lo Basic + DA Rs 25,000 hai to dono taraf se Rs 3,000 jama hota hai · Samjho company ka hissa salary se nahi kat-ta — woh alag se jata hai, usme se 8.33% pension mein aur 3.67% PF mein · Interest har saal judta hai — passbook mein balance badhta dekho, aur poora PF naukri chhodne ke 12 mahine baad milega
- **Cover:** PF KYA HAI? 12% + 12%

## ep01-claim-reject

- **Title:** PF claim reject ho gaya? Wajah aur fix.
- **Topic:** PF claim reject kyun hota hai, aur kaise theek karein?
- **Hook:** PF claim reject ho gaya? Paisa atka hai?
- **Covers:** Why: Filed before 12 months without a job: the rule since the EPF Scheme 2026 · The name or date of birth doesn't match Aadhaar · The date of exit isn't in EPFO's records. Steps: Read the reason in the claim status · Correct the name or date of birth under Manage, then Modify Basic Details (no document if the UAN is Aadhaar-verified) · Mark the date of exit, 60 days after leaving · File Form 19 again once 12 months are done
